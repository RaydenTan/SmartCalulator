1. Basic Calculations & Roots

  • Roots: sqrt(16) -> 4 or cbrt(27) -> 3
  • Standard Math: 12 + 8 * (10 - 2) / 4
  • Implicit Typing: 4x = 16 or 8(x + 2) = 32

2. Algebra & Calculus

  • Algebra Equations: x^2 = 4 or 2x + y = 5, x - y = 1
  • Derivatives: diff(x^3 + sin(x), x)
  • Integrals: integrate(x^2, x)

3. Statistics & Matrices

  • Statistics: mean(2, 4, 6, 8) or median(1, 3, 5)
  • Determinants: det([[7, 8], [9, 10]])
