1. Basic Calculations & Roots

  • Standard Math: 12 + 8 * (10 - 2) / 4
  • Roots: sqrt(16) or cbrt(27)
  • Powers / Exponents: 5² or 2^4

2. Trigonometry

  • Functions: sin(30), cos(60), tan(45)
  • Inverse Trig: asin(0.5), acos(0.5), atan(1)

3. Algebra Solver (SymPy)

  • Single Equations: 4x = 16 or x^2 = 4
  • Equation Systems: 2x + y = 5, x - y = 1

4. Calculus (SymPy Engine)

  • Derivatives: diff(x^3 + sin(x), x)
  • Indefinite Integrals: integrate(x^2, x)
  • Definite Integrals: integrate(x^3 + sin(x), (x, 0, 1))
  • Limits: limit(sin(x)/x, x, 0)
  • Series Expansion: series(exp(x), x, 0, 4)

5. Matrix Algebra

  • Determinant: det([,])
  • Inverse Matrix: inv([,])

6. Statistics

  • Mean / Average: mean(2, 4, 6, 8)
  • Median: median(1, 3, 5, 7, 9)
  • Standard Deviation: stdev(2, 4, 4, 5, 7, 9)
